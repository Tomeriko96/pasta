New to Italian food? You are in the right place. We write each dish in Italian, just as we serve it. The little notes below say what is in it, in plain English. Not sure what to pick? Please ask, we are happy to help.
A quick guide: Bruschetta (broo-SKET-ta) is toasted bread with toppings. Rucola is rocket (arugula). Cozze (KOT-seh) are mussels. Spicy means a little chilli heat.
All our pasta is made fresh. Buon appetito, and welcome.
